Hatusa privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Hatusa runs on your PC. Hatusa itself makes no network connections, has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry, and sends no data to the developer.

Windows components

Hatusa's window is drawn by Microsoft Edge WebView2, a part of Windows. Like other Windows components, WebView2 sends required diagnostic data to Microsoft regardless of your settings, and optional diagnostic data only if you allow it in your Windows diagnostic data settings; this is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement, not by Hatusa. In the Microsoft Store version, Windows checks your license with the Microsoft Store service; Hatusa itself still makes no network connections. Links in Hatusa (this policy, the support page) open in your web browser.

What Hatusa stores on your PC

To remember your choices, Hatusa keeps a settings file in its app data folder. It contains monitor identifiers (built from each monitor's manufacturer, model and, when available, serial number, as reported by the monitor), the names you give to monitors and inputs, your brightness schedule and your preferences. If you turn on the optional error log (off by default), warnings and errors are written to a log file of at most 2 MB (Microsoft Store version: in the app's own package folder; other version: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Hatusa\logs).

What leaves your PC

Nothing is sent by Hatusa. "Copy diagnostics" copies a report to your clipboard; it includes monitor models, connection types and DDC/CI responses, with serial numbers masked, and, if the error log is on, its most recent part. It is shared only if you paste it somewhere yourself, for example in a support request — support requests on GitHub are public.

Your control

You can change or delete names and preferences in Hatusa at any time. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version removes the settings and logs kept in its package folder. If the other (non-Store) version of Hatusa was installed on the same PC before, its settings file is used by both and stays after uninstalling.

Children

Hatusa does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
